A2. GRAVE-VIGIL GREY PASSAGE — step-rule violation (After-Liwon vigil)
Pre-existing text our straggler sweep missed; the only remaining
soul-drift in the book: "The grey came and sat with him sometimes —
he could reach it without moving now" (the grey comes to him; he
reaches it without stepping; "stayed in it" disembodied).

FIND:
  The grey came and sat with him sometimes — he could reach it without
  moving now, the warm part, the part where she still got the rhyme
  right — and he stayed in it longer and longer, and minded less and
  less the coming back,

REPLACE WITH (recommended — keeps the dying-by-preference arc;
brief bodily steps, 100x grey-time):
  He stepped to the grey more and more — a breath gone from the
  hillside, a long while on the far side of it, the warm part, the
  part where she still got the rhyme right — and he stayed longer and
  longer inside each breath, and minded less and less the coming back,

ALTERNATIVE (if you'd rather he never goes during the vigil — strongest
fit with the more-shrub-than-statue overgrowth, but changes meaning):
  The grey pulled at him more and more — she was in it, the warm part,
  the part where she still got the rhyme right — and he leaned into
  the pull longer and longer, and minded less and less how little held
  him on this side,
