# FRD — Acts

The book is divided into three named acts. Chapters keep their numbers; only the acts have titles.
`join.py` and the book build both read this file. One act per line: name | title | first chapter | epigraph (Titanspeech root) | epigraph gloss.
The last two fields are optional; `join.py` prints them as a centred line under the act heading.

Act One | The Quiet Town | 1 | VDF | peace
Act Two | Where the Lines Point | 10 | DPJ | anti-spinward
Act Three | The Open Book | 32 | BHZ | open
