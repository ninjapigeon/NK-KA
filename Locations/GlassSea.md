## The Glass Sea

A roughly circular expanse of smoky, glasslike black material — easily mistaken for obsidian — surrounding the Spire for about five kilometers (a quarter-day’s travel) in every direction. The city of Dunarth sits at one edge of it, the last real stop for anyone foolhardy enough to be heading toward the Spire itself.

Despite its name, the Glass Sea is bone dry, and aggressively so — anyone spending real time inside it needs to watch their water supply carefully, both while moving and while camped, since the place does nothing to help and a great deal to hurt. At night, without real protection, the cold here is enough to leave a person dangerously hypothermic. The “glass” itself seems to repair small damage to its surface on its own, given time.

Skybarges cannot cross it — something about the place is flatly hostile to whatever keeps a barge in the air — and any magefire-powered device carried across visibly loses power the deeper in it goes, Boltcasters included. The same goes for anything relying on a magefire battery to keep functioning at all: this much raw, ungrounded Ptah in one place seems to overwhelm batteries entirely rather than merely draining them, and whatever they’re powering simply stops. For a Forged — whose chest batteries are the only thing keeping them going — crossing the Glass Sea isn’t a risk. It’s a death sentence, and reason enough for even the oldest and most curious of them to give the place a permanent, respectful distance.
