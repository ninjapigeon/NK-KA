## The Blood Geysers  

Scattered across Anka — never staying in the same place for long, in the grand scheme of things — are the geyser fields: places where wet titan blood reaches the surface and doesn’t stop. Each working geyser builds itself a tapering, termite-mound chimney of dried blood as it sprays, while the wet blood that falls short pools at the base. Those pools feed an overgrowth of strange, often dangerous flora unique to titan-blood-soaked ground — left unchecked, a geyser chokes itself in its own garden within a season, which is why every working site needs someone willing to keep cutting the growth back.

The wet blood from these pools is the raw material behind Hexwood, Greensteel, and the refined-blood gambles people still take for a burst of strength (see Titan Blood) — which makes a healthy, cleared geyser field a genuinely valuable place to be, if you can stomach the work and the company the overgrowth tends to attract.

Geysers don’t last forever. When one finally dies — whatever artery feeds it runs dry, or seals over — the site goes quiet, and what’s left dries down to something black and glassy. Enough dead sites, linked up across enough distance and time, are what most people are really looking at when they look at the cracks that scar Anka, and the Glass Sea itself.
