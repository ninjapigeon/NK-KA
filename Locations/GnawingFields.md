## The Gnawing Fields

At a glance, these are simply fields — yellow grass, sometimes shading to red, heather growing wild. What sets them apart is what’s scattered through the grass: squared-off white plinths, none taller than a person, some no higher than a knee, in no pattern anyone’s identified, for height or placement either. Touch one and it’s warm, even on a cold day.

These are the glutton’s teeth — pulled, or knocked loose, sometime during the Titan war, and left exactly where they landed ever since. Don’t call them gravestones. People who do tend to assume some connection to the Giant’s Graveyard, and there isn’t one; the two places share a color palette and nothing else.

Fist-sized chunks of debris drift among the plinths in loose, slow clusters of about a dozen — floating, with no visible support, the way nothing else on Anka does. This is floatstone, and it’s a genuine local curiosity: a piece will pull away from any nearby vein of black glass as if repelled by it, which is what keeps it aloft. A whole town at the rim of the Fields lives on harvesting and selling it as novelties and charms — but it’s a curiosity, not an industry. Skybarges fly on fans and arcane batteries, same as everything else; floatstone has never scaled into anything bigger than a trinket.

> [!tag]- Secret

The real danger here isn’t the plinths — it’s what moves between them. Grinding Teeth (see Fauna) are drawn to motion, and the Gnawing Fields are full of them, mostly indistinguishable from the floating debris until they’re already converging. The only reliable defense is the one nobody wants to rely on: stay still, and they lose interest.
