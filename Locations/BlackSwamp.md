## The Black Swamp

What would otherwise be an ordinary forest sits just below the waterline here, turning the whole area into a slow, standing bog. The water that keeps it that way runs off from the nearest geyser field — close enough that the swamp never quite dries out, far enough that most people never make the connection.

> [!tag]- Secret

The swamp is home to more than frogs and rot. Something in the standing water and the still air settles into a sleeping poison, thick enough that anyone spending real time in the swamp — on arrival, and again for every day they stay — breathes in enough of it to feel the effects: a creeping drowsiness that, left unchecked, can tip into something much deeper. The swamp is also, by every account, home to the restless dead — exactly the kind of place an outbreak of the wandering kind gets its start, if it hasn’t already. Antidotes to the sleeping poison work, though even a successful dose leaves the taker a little duller for it; taken ahead of time, the same antidote holds off a day’s exposure entirely.
