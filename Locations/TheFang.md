THE FANG

The venom-aspect's instrument — Queen of Serpents, Mother of Venom. A single titanic fang, hollow, buried point-down somewhere beneath the Black Swamp. Still faintly weeping after 300,000 spins.

The slow seep is the true cause of the swamp's ambient poison. The standing-water-and-still-air explanation locals give isn't wrong, exactly — the water and the air are how the poison spreads — it's just incomplete. The water and air are the delivery system. The Fang is the source. Nobody currently living knows this, including anyone in Book 1 or Book 2.
