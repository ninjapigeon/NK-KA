## The Emperor’s Causeway

From a distance, this is just a very long bridge. Up close, it’s almost absurdly oversized: a roadway wide enough for fifteen Humins to walk shoulder to shoulder, carried on arches that average six Humins high. Every thousand paces or so, an arch above the roadway opens onto a corkscrew ramp leading down to the ground — these are the only way on or off, since the bridge itself has no other access points along its length. The roadway-side gates over these ramps still have working clockwork chain mechanisms; the ground-side ramps have no gates at all.

It’s commonly said the Causeway was built during the Titan war, to move large bodies of titan-spawn troops quickly between fronts. The trouble is that it doesn’t connect to anywhere any historian can place as a front, and titan-spawn rarely moved in groups organized enough for a bridge like this to matter militarily. Most people who’ve thought about it longer than a few minutes settle on the same conclusion: it’s a Titan creation, built the way the Titan built everything — because it could, not because it needed to.

The bridge's name is older than any of these theories, and outlasts them. "Emperor" refers to Acnash, first of the Giants to steal magic from the Titans and, in an age before Virek, Kro, Ork, or Humins existed, Emperor over his own kind. Whether the Titan raised the Causeway at Acnash's request, in his memory, or without any thought of him at all — the way it built everything — is a question nobody living can answer. What's left is only the name, worn onto a road that predates every people currently walking across it.
