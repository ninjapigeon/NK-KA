## Terminology & voice
- [stated] "Dark phase" is the in-world term, kept in Third's grey-memory register and in-world dialogue; "night" is used in Tasha's narration.
- [stated] The weapon is the Boltcaster (earlier names Boltcaster, then Boltcaster — both retired); the climax beat is "THE BOLTCASTER NEVER FIRES" — Dorn's Boltcaster is never fired and is left at the foot of the spine (ch39).
- [stated] Magefire is a Kef-only manufacturing secret sold through the Standards Guild (formerly "Spellfire Union").
- [stated] "Titanspawn Forged" — the Forged is a class of Titanspawn, not the reverse.
- [stated] The people "Keth" are renamed "Kef" (Titanspeech has no TH digraph — one letter, F, covers that range; written root KTH becomes KF). Every prose instance of "Keth" needs updating to "Kef," including "Keth-only manufacturing secret" and "Keth-invented Serpent Scale" below.
- [stated] Titanspeech root GNM = Hin, doubling as "nameless" (a Hin has no name until a story makes them real — mirrors BKR's Titan/power doubling). Reversal MNG = "named" / "told-into-being," Titanspeech's closest word for a storyteller or author.

## Third
- [stated] Third is shorter than average Virek (~human height); his wrongness comes from anatomical details, not scale.
- [stated] Third has cooked for others (Liwon) but never lit a fire for his own benefit — a meaningful continuity distinction.
- [stated] Third's original armor is "Titan-willed articulated plating," explicitly unrelated to Kef-invented Serpent Scale.
- [stated] Third runs on two Titan-original pickle-jar-sized (double-wire) containment vessels.
- [stated] The Glaive is Titan-made, pale/bone-coloured — not wood; described as "pale blade". Third leaves it at the brook beside the Virek boy on his first day and never retrieves it (ch16, ch40); his weapons thereafter are the self-made Falkas.

## Materials & technology
- [stated] Jade is a stone with oxidized copper's color and copper's ductility — not chemically copper; Titan-blood anointing only poisons jade, it does not harden it.
- [stated] Metalworking on Anka uses copper only (no bronze/tin); Numberlock Brass is retired.
- [stated] Hexwood is strictly post-Titansfall; Teeth are stamped ordinary wood then anointed with wet Titan blood to become Hexwood.
- [stated] Skybarge lift uses fans plus racked arcane batteries (floatstone demoted to a Gnawing Fields curiosity).
- [stated] Glassvine (renamed from Razorvine): restrains stationary objects, releases glass splinters when cut.
- [stated] Anka never developed longbows or crossbows; Boltcasters pre-empted the arms-race niche. Kern's bow is compact/recurved with Powder Charter / 200,000-spin obsolescence.
- [stated] Dreadnoughts are attracted to cooked food and cooking fires, not worked metal.
- [stated] The Scar's fuzzy-edge transition is 5 minutes, not 30.

## Institutions & history
- [stated] The Standards Guild and Powder Charter are the same institution at two points in history.
- [stated] Acnash (a cyclopskin Giant) was the first to steal magic from the Titans, became Emperor, and is the referent for the Emperor's Causeway; other giants stole from Acnash's work to create the Virek.

## Era 2 timeline

## Book 1 manuscript canon (Oct 2026 — the finished chapters win)
- [stated] The book is titled FRD (Titanspeech abjad for Third); "Third" remains the character's name.
- [stated] Falka(s) (capital F) are Third's self-made blades; the sundered peoples are Kro and Ork; Causeway and Titan are always capitalised.
- [stated] Third leaves the glaive at the brook beside the Virek boy with the bow on the day he first speaks, and never retrieves it; at the end he leaves Dorn's Boltcaster at the foot of the spine and keeps the Falkas.
- [stated] Dorn's joke is a hide-and-seek joke (a Mender and a Crafter play to settle whose work matters; the Grask is "It"); its punchline is "Ready or not." The real Grask says "Found you."
- [stated] Giant's Graveyard: cliff ~100 m high; slab field ~5 km from the cliff face to the last fallen slab, ~300 m wide; slabs 10-20 m across and up to ~100 m high.
- [stated] The Causeway's corkscrew ramps are two thousand paces apart.
- [stated] Sorcerers can reach Akna with a spell, at a cost; people stumble in at thin places; Sougad crosses by saying an old word and is not spent by it; Third needs no word and pays no price.
- [stated] Chipwood: small settlement two days spinward of Dunarth; the traveller's home in ch6; not the town of ch17.
- [stated] Minutes, seconds and hours are acceptable in-world.
- [stated] Sougad is first named in ch36; the Anvil in ch35; "the cold man" means Sougad from ch33.
- [stated] Third's eye is blue. Roan favours his left leg (ch1) and limps on the other leg after the climax (ch41).
- [stated] The Porter lowers a ramp, never ropes; it holds at the rim because no barge-master brings a hull down to board under falling rock (ch41).
