> ## TITANSPEECH — PHONOLOGY
>
> Simplified digraph mapping — how the abjad is actually spoken
> and written, by everyone, always. Not a separate ritual register;
> this is ordinary Titanspeech phonology.
>
> - TH (voiceless, as in "think") -> **F**
> - TH (voiced, as in "this") -> **V**
> - SH -> **X**
> - CH -> **J**
>
> *(Does not affect the consonant-root glossary above — a root like
> KTH is three separate letters, K-T-H, not "K" plus a TH digraph.
> This rule only applies where an actual TH/SH/CH sound would
> otherwise occur.)*
>
> ## TITANSPEECH — WORDS AS POWER (locked)
>
> Titanspeech is not two languages, one plain and one magical — it
> is one language, spoken descriptively by nearly everyone, and
> performatively by almost no one. The roots aren't arbitrary
> labels; they're compressed structural claims about how reality is
> actually built (Akna genuinely IS the reversed-opposite of Anka —
> NK/KN is not metaphor, it's description). For most speakers that
> structural truth stays inert: saying a root describes, the way
> "fire" doesn't burn the mouth that says it. For a being with the
> right standing — proximity to whatever actually authored that
> structure — the same root stops describing and starts doing.
>
> This is a performative utterance, not a spell-language: the
> identical word, said by two different speakers, can be empty in
> one mouth and load-bearing in the other, with no difference in
> the word itself. Standing is earned, not learned. Sougad's
> command of KT/TK (the Roan-fling, Part Two) comes from having
> lived on both sides of the solid/nether split those roots encode
> — banished into Akna itself, over a century of compressed
> subjective time before the Anvil even arrived. He is not casting.
> He is the rare case where saying is doing.
